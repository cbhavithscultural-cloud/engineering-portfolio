I’m C. Bhavith Sai, a final-year BTech Mechatronics Engineering student working toward a career in embedded systems engineering. I’m drawn to systems where electronics, firmware, mechanics and algorithms have to work together — especially robotics, sensing and biomedical applications.

This portfolio is a working record of projects, experiments and engineering decisions. I aim to show not only what a system does, but how it is built, what was measured and what still needs improvement.
